# Power BI Sales Analysis with DAX

## 📊 Project Overview

This project explores sales performance using Microsoft Power BI and DAX (Data Analysis Expressions). It focuses on time-based calculations, sales commission logic, cumulative sales, and quarterly performance comparisons.

The goal is to transform sales data into meaningful business metrics and present them through clear, interactive visualizations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze sales revenue over time.
- Calculate year-to-date (YTD) sales metrics.
- Evaluate sales commission rates by quarter.
- Track cumulative sales performance.
- Compare current results with previous periods.
- Analyze quarterly sales and product quantity performance.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** — report development and visualization
- **DAX** — measures and business calculations
- **Time Intelligence** — year-to-date and period-over-period analysis
- **Data Visualization** — tables, matrices, KPI indicators, and conditional formatting

## 📈 Key Analysis Areas

### 1. Sales Revenue YTD Share %

Analyze sales from the beginning of the year and their share of the relevant annual sales total, with a breakdown by year and month.

### 2. Sales Commission Analysis

Calculate sales commissions using quarter-specific rates:

- Q1 — 10%
- Q2 — 11%
- Q3 — 12%
- Q4 — 15%

This helps demonstrate how business rules can be incorporated into analytical measures.

### 3. Running Total

Calculate cumulative sales to track how the total develops over time.

### 4. Average Sales

Calculate average sales for the most recent six-month period and apply conditional formatting to make the results easier to interpret.

### 5. Percentage Change vs. Previous Period

Calculate percentage changes relative to a previous record or period to help identify changes in sales performance.

### 6. Quarterly Performance

Compare quarterly performance using current-quarter and previous-quarter measures, including sales values and product quantities.

## 💡 Business Value

The analysis demonstrates how Power BI and DAX can support:

- Monitoring sales trends over time.
- Evaluating commission structures.
- Tracking cumulative performance.
- Identifying changes between periods.
- Comparing quarterly results.
- Presenting key metrics in an accessible format.
